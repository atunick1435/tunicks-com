The Russian full name involves three parts: first (given) name, surname and patronymics. Patronymics consists of the possessive form of the father's first name with suffixes. Males attach "ovich" or "evich" and females attach "ovna" or "evna" depending on which works better with their father's first name. For example Ivan Alexandrovich is translated, Ivan, son of Alexander. Maria Alexandrovna, is translated, Maria daughter of Alexander. In the pre-revolution era officials used mostly the short form of patronymics which is a possessive form itself, in the case of women it ends with a (Ivan Alexandrov, Maria Alexandrova).
